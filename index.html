What is Aspect Ratio?

Aspect ratio is the relationship between the width and height of a video or image. In Alight Motion, choosing the correct aspect ratio helps your videos fit perfectly on social media platforms.

Why Aspect Ratios Matter

Using the wrong aspect ratio can create black bars, cropped visuals, or stretched content. Professional editors using Alight Motion always select the correct format before editing.

  • Improve viewer experience
  • Make videos look professional
  • Optimize content for mobile screens
  • Avoid cropping important content

Common Aspect Ratios

1. 16:9 Aspect Ratio

Best for YouTube videos, gaming, tutorials, and widescreen content.

2. 9:16 Aspect Ratio

Perfect for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

3. 1:1 Aspect Ratio

Used for Instagram square posts and promotional content.

4. 4:5 Aspect Ratio

Ideal for Instagram and Facebook mobile feeds.

How to Set Aspect Ratio in Alight Motion

  1. Open Alight Motion
  2. Tap the Plus (+) button
  3. Create a new project
  4. Choose width and height
  5. Select your preferred aspect ratio
  6. Start editing

Best Aspect Ratios for Platforms

Platform Recommended Ratio
YouTube 16:9
Instagram Reels 9:16
TikTok 9:16
YouTube Shorts 9:16
Instagram Feed 1:1 or 4:5

Tips for Beginners

  • Decide the platform before editing
  • Keep important content in the center
  • Use high-resolution exports
  • Avoid changing aspect ratio midway
  • Preview videos before uploading

Final Thoughts

Understanding aspect ratios in Alight Motion can greatly improve your editing quality. Choosing the correct ratio helps your videos look cleaner, more professional, and optimized for social media platforms.
